How HIREWORLD works when fully built

Every imported job keeps its source, original link and last-checked time. Missing details stay “unknown”. Duplicates across platforms are merged only when confidence is high; uncertain ones go to a human reviewer. A failed request never closes a job on its own.

You can report an incorrect listing using the “Report” button on any card; reports are saved locally in this demo.
